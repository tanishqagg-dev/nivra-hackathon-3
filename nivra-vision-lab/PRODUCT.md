# NIVRA Vision Lab

## Register

Product

## Users

Public-space guards, school safety staff, and civic control-room operators who need to understand a live scene quickly without treating an automated model as the final authority.

## Product Purpose

NIVRA turns local camera input into explainable, time-based observations that help a human operator review developing situations earlier. The browser lab proves the on-device vision layer without recording footage, identifying people, or making claims of guilt.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, humane, and operational. NIVRA should feel like trustworthy public infrastructure: quiet by default, immediately legible under pressure, and deliberate when human attention is needed.

## Anti-References

- Hacker HUDs, neon surveillance theatrics, and dense command-center chrome
- Red-alert dashboards that keep everything in a permanent state of alarm
- Crime, guilt, intent, or identity labels inferred from a camera frame
- Playful hackathon styling, generic SaaS card grids, and decorative metrics
- Hidden cloud uploads, permanent person IDs, or unexplained risk scores

## Design Principles

1. **Sequence over screenshot.** Describe observable state changes such as carried, separated, transferred, or unattended.
2. **Camera first.** Attach information to the live scene and keep secondary evidence in a narrow observation rail.
3. **Explain what changed.** Every review event names the visible signal and avoids claims about intent.
4. **Quiet is healthy.** NIVRA Blue communicates normal analysis; Intervention Coral appears only when human review is useful.
5. **Human authority.** The operator verifies every flagged observation and remains responsible for any response.
6. **Local and minimal.** Process frames on-device, retain no video, and use only temporary in-session tracks.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard operation.
- Never use color as the only indicator of state.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid urgency animation loops.
- Use direct, readable language suitable for stressed operators.
- Do not infer identity, demographic traits, disability, intent, or criminality.
